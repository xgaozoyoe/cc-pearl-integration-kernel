Cross-K delayed reduction, not whole-fold deferral.

Historical crossloop experiment delayed reading C itself until next K. Historical
handoff experiment moved whole fold after next copy. This experiment instead
reads all C elements at their correct boundary and saves only local XOR for N
last-row tiles. No full accumulator snapshot, no altered MMA arithmetic.

Each warp holds N local partials per lane (N registers/thread before compiler
allocation), not N values total across the warp.
At boundary k=1,3,...63, localXOR of row3/groups(4-N)..3 is saved; other tiles fold
normally. At next k=2,4,...62, deferred REDUX and transcript update consume saved
partials. New MMA can safely overwrite C because its old values have been read.
Final k=63 pending results drain after loop, before original CTA output barrier.
rb at drain is k/2-1; final drain rb31. First16folds store, next16rotate13+xor.

native: unchanged current best8+4+fold-preamble compact cubin.
source8: source recompile, same unsigned index and8+4 handoff, no delayed result.
defer2_copy: REDUX/store of2tiles after next iteration copy commit, beforeLDSM.
defer2_mma: REDUX/store of2tiles after first two rows' second IMMA in next K.
defer4_mma: REDUX/store of4tiles after first four rows' second IMMA in next K.
defer1_mma: narrow follow-up, one tile after first row's second IMMA.

SASS confirms:
- Every source variant has64staticIMMA and16LDSM.
- defer2_copy: LDGDEPBAR0xef0 then prior-result REDUX0x1010, before firstLDSM0x1350.
- defer2_mma: firstIMMA0x1210/0x1230, prior-result REDUX0x12a0.
- defer4_mma: firstIMMA0x1230/0x1250, prior-result REDUX0x12c0.
- Prior local XOR carried in registers across backedge; see sass-audit.txt.
This proves instruction interleaving, not measured cycle-level simultaneous issue.

Material confound: source recompilation sinks BAR.ARV to end of MMA, unlike native's
16remainingIMMA. source8 has same publication limitation and gives an additional
comparison. These are implementable prototypes, not fixed-SASS single-factor ablations.
