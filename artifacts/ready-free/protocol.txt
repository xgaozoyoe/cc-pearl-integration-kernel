Isolated 2-slot ready/free experiment (SM89,384threads,all warps still compute).
32 extra shared bytes: ready0,ready1,free0,free1; aligned8,count384 each.

For logical tile k: slot=k%2,phase=(k/2)%2.
Producer: wait free(slot,k-2) before overwriting, except virgin k0/k1;
issue unchanged cp.async copies;enqueue ONE async ready arrival/thread.
Consumer: wait ready(slot,k);load fragments/compute;arrive free(slot,k)
after old-stage reads;compute transcript;proceed without waiting for peers' fold.
Ready uses cp.async.mbarrier.arrive.noinc,not an additional normal arrive.
Free uses one mbarrier.arrive perthread. Waiting does not contribute arrival.

Induction:
Initial slots pristine. Ready(k) cannot complete until all384 producers' copies
complete, and successful test_wait acquires their publication before LDSM.
Free(k) cannot complete until all384 consumers' reads precede their release.
Every writer(k+2) observes free(k), so cannot overwrite unread k.
Before ready(k+2) can receive arrivals,free(k) must complete;all readers(k)
observed ready(k), so earlier ready phase has been observed.
Before free(k+2) can receive arrivals,ready(k+2) must complete;all writers(k+2)
observed free(k), so earlier free phase has been observed.
No waiter can lag across2 completed phases:its own required contribution to
the next use prevents the second completion. Parity therefore remains valid.
Final CTA barrier retained;all copies needed by final reads have been observed.
Objects invalidated only after final CTA rendezvous. No live mining entrypoint.

split_late: waits free(current),ready(next) at end before next iteration.
split_deferred: waits free only before overwrite and ready only before reads.
Both preserve copy-before-compute order. A future experiment changing that order
would be distinct and is not claimed here.

The source snapshot publishes after final MMA when compiled. Fast frozen SASS
publishes after48of64MMA. This difference is a confound; native and recompiled
source_control are both measured. Compiler scheduling differences prevent
attributing total time differences solely to barrier instruction overhead.

Random model uses12 warp-aggregated participants and random completion delays;
100schedules/mode;not exhaustive GPU scheduling proof. Negative controls remove
ready/free waiting and must detect unready read/overwrite respectively.
CUDA transcript/PoW validation and racecheck are separate empirical checks.

Expected new costs (measure before accepting):two wait loops persteadyiteration,
phase/address arithmetic,async publication,free shared atomic arrival and fence.
No extra MMA/copy work,no extra warp,no intermediate accumulator spill required.
