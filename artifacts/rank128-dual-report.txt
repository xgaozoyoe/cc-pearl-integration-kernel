Conclusion: dualCTA improves scheduling availability and reported barrier stalls,
but the smaller CTA/warp mapping increases data movement per useful MMA and is
slower in both profiling and independent end-to-end benchmarks. Extra on-chip
traffic is a measured regression, though these counters cannot assign every lost
millisecond to traffic alone. Do not deploy this candidate; retain singleCTA.
Next work should preserve current operand reuse while examining main-loop address,
copy issue and LDSM/IMMA instruction scheduling, rather than occupancy alone.
