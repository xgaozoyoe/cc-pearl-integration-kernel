Critical limits:
1.RZ substitutes arithmetic operands but original scoreboard wait masks
  and fixed stall counts are retained.A/B tests therefore do NOT fully
  remove LDSM readiness waits.C-zero also retains compiler-inserted timing.
  Small/no speedup cannot rule out a dependency whose encoded wait remains.
2.Zeroing changes arithmetic switching,register reads and power.Baseline
  block-end SM clocks2580..2715MHz;C-zero2520..2715;A/B-zero mostly2715.
  Clock settings were unchanged,not locked.Power/state changes can explain
  part or all of the apparent operand-probe gains;no causal fraction claimed.
3.This is NOT evidence of5-6% available correct-mining optimization.
4.No new hardware counters yet.This first round verifies a controlled
  machine-code mutation method and supplies timings,not final attribution.
