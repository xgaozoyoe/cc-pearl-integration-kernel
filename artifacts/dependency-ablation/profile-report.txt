Conclusions:
- Substituting C alone gives little cycle-throughput benefit under the
  current schedule.It does not support a large immediately exposed C RAW
  latency bottleneck,but retained fixed stalls prevent absolute exclusion.
- A/B substitution alone also does not substantially raise Tensor density.
- This does NOT falsify the load-dependency hypothesis:the experiment did
  not remove explicit scoreboard waits or compiler-inserted stall counts.
- The prior5-6%wall-time gains cannot be advertised as dependency-removal
  gains or a usable correct-mining optimization.

Next experimental requirement:decode producer write/read barriers and
consumer wait masks;only remove/relocate waits rendered unnecessary by
operand substitution while preserving outstanding RAW/WAR/WAW constraints
on later instructions,pointer reuse and shared-memory lifetime. Blindly
zeroing all wait masks or deleting synchronization is not a valid control.
No such scheduling-bit edits were made in this round.
