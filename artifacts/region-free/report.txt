GPU0-only region-reuse experiment; no mining backend replacement.
8192x8192 region,K4096,rank128,CTA256x192,384threads,K64,2stages.
Two independent runs,40CUDA-event samples/variant/run,16launches/sample;3second warmup plus100launch warmups per block. Unlocked clocks;end-of-block telemetry in logs.
NOT full-grid wall time or pool hashrate. No hardware-counter measurements.

Protocol:2whole-stage ready mbarriers(count384),14free mbarriers(7regions x2slots). A4regions eachcount96;B3regions eachcount128. Every warp publishes its A/B region read completion via2arrivals. Per-copy free waits use old-stage parity((copiedK-2)/2)&1;ready wait remains before MMA.
early_wait and deferred use the same logical regions/arrival counts/wait calls:early waits for all4or5destination regions before copying;deferred interleaves each wait with its corresponding copy. Compiler schedules differ, so not an instruction-identical latency test.
Phase reuse is bounded by whole-stage ready rendezvous:all384threads must publish intervening-stage copies before all readers can progress through another reuse of the same slot. No unrestricted producer lap is allowed.
