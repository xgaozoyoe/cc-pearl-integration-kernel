MMA work while A is folded. No full-matrix/CTA/warp remapping was attempted.

v1 group8 steady sequence: B_fragment_i MMA pair -> complete A_fragment_i fold.

Conclusion:both tested interleavings lose~8% throughput. Moving fold instructions
between independent MMA fragments alone is insufficient; extra control flow and
changed scheduling are real confounders. No new counter capture isolates their
individual cost. Do not claim all possible group pipelines are impossible,or
attribute the entire regression to nine reconvergence sites. The proposed limited
prototype does not justify further expansion. Preserve current baseline.
Production and A-stream candidate hashes verified unchanged,no live mining,
no commits,isolated libraries only. Per-mode source diffs retained for review.
